Activation rule

At least 40% lower p95, sustained

For each of three consecutive, non-overlapping five-minute windows, the candidate p95 must be at most 60% of baseline p95. Each window needs at least 60 successful paired baseline/candidate samples, and candidate p99 latency and packet loss must be no worse. Fresh verified evidence from distinct physical endpoints is required before activating accelerated transport. A failed active window or restart returns the session to baseline.
