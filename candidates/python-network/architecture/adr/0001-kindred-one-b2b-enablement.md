# ADR 0001: Kindred One B2B enablement

Status: Accepted by authoritative correction; implementation remains evidence-gated.

Kindred One transitions from consumer-targeted product positioning to a B2B
enablement and infrastructure platform. Organizations and authorized partners
are the principal commercial and operational context. Human-facing experiences
remain partner-admin, organizational-user, operator, embedded, white-label, or
partner-controlled downstream interfaces.
