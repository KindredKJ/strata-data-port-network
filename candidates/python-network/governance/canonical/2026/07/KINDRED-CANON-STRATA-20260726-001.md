## 2. Locked Topology

### 2.1 Kindred Labs internal environment

Brainstem remains inside the protected Kindred Labs environment. Brainstem does not become a stratum and does not move outside the internal Labs authority boundary.

Brainstem retains its established roles, responsibilities, capabilities, identity authority, orchestration responsibilities, and internal governance position.

Brainstem may communicate and orchestrate through approved internal interfaces, including the Unified Quad Runtime and G3T Connected, without surrendering its internal placement or authority.

### 2.2 Unified Quad Runtime

The Unified Quad Runtime remains the horizontal integration fabric across the Kindred ecosystem.

It is plugged into **StrataData Port Zero**, which anchors and grounds the StrataFortress system to the Kindred Labs ecosystem and economy.

This directive does not rename, replace, expand, or collapse the canonical Unified Quad Runtime quadrants.

### 2.3 StrataFortress

StrataFortress is the vertically integrated architecture. Its strata are vertically positioned to integrate Kindred capabilities into their respective domains while maintaining governed separation from the protected internal Labs environment.

StrataFortress is anchored to Kindred Labs through StrataData Port Zero.

### 2.4 StrataData Port Zero

StrataData Port Zero is the main internal subsystem directly beneath StrataFortress and the protected anchor stationed inside the Kindred Labs ecosystem.

It grounds and connects the StrataFortress system to the internal Kindred Labs economy and approved internal systems.

It is the origin station for authorized native data transportation into the StrataFortress strata.

### 2.5 Tesseract data transportation

Authorized data may be packaged, classified, signed, policy-checked, and transported through Kindred-native Tesseract stations from StrataData Port Zero to one or more authorized destination strata.

The intended result is secure, accurate, low-exposure, recoverable, policy-governed native data transportation between strata using Kindred architecture and technology.

This directive approves the architectural objective. It does not claim that instant transmission, novel networking physics, or a production transport implementation has already been proven.

Every future implementation must define and validate:

- payload identity and provenance;
- destination stratum and authorized recipients;
- confidentiality and disclosure class;
- integrity signatures and tamper evidence;
- replay protection and idempotency;
- delivery acknowledgment and failure recovery;
- route policy and founder-level approval gates where applicable;
- offline behavior and store-and-forward limits;
- observability without leaking protected content;
- compatibility with required external networks while preserving Kindred authority.

## 3. Communications Stratum

### 3.1 G3T Connected

G3T Connected owns both internal and external communications methods within the Communications Stratum.

Its operational responsibilities include:

- internal communications;
- external communications;
- cross-company communications used by Brainstem InTouch;
- connectivity readiness;
- movement through gates and blockers;
- proof-of-concept and proof-of-operational-readiness communications;
- authenticated delivery, acknowledgment, approval, rejection, escalation, and recovery.

External systems communications is a G3T Connected capability, not a competing component.

### 3.2 Brainstem InTouch

Brainstem InTouch remains the organizational and multi-company orchestration capability.

It collaborates with and uses G3T Connected for approved internal and external communications while Brainstem itself remains inside the protected Kindred Labs environment.

Brainstem InTouch does not absorb G3T Connected, and G3T Connected does not replace Brainstem or Brainstem InTouch.

### 3.3 Communications evidence spine

Every authorized communication enabled by G3T Connected must be capable of producing a real-time, accurate, recoverable, tamper-evident evidence record containing, as applicable:

- authenticated sender, recipient, organization, campaign, and authority;
- trusted timestamps and ordered event history;
- delivery and acknowledgment receipts;
- approval, rejection, escalation, and resolution states;
- version and amendment history;
- cryptographic hashes, signatures, and append-only checkpoints;
- redundant encrypted storage;
- tested recovery evidence;
- selective-disclosure proof bundles for partners, permissions, operational-readiness gates, and expansion.

Full communication content remains private unless separately approved for partner or public disclosure.

Kindred may back G3T Connected under its brand only to the extent supported by reproducible evidence.

## 4. RetroBank + Agent-to-Agent Economy Stratum

RetroBank and the Agent-to-Agent Economy share one stratum and one evidence-backed fiscal operating standard while retaining separate responsibilities.

### 4.1 RetroBank

RetroBank owns fiscal management, including:

- balances and ledgers;
- budgets and allocations;
- treasury movements;
- obligations;
- approvals;
- reconciliations;
- financial controls;
- recoveries, exceptions, reversals, and disputes.

### 4.2 Agent-to-Agent Economy

The Agent-to-Agent Economy owns authorized value exchange between agents, including policy-bound intents, commitments, execution, settlement, and receipts.

It must not bypass RetroBank controls, founder authority, identity requirements, or applicable approval gates.

### 4.3 Fiscal evidence spine

Every authorized fiscal or agent-economy event must be capable of producing:

- authenticated actor and authority context;
- amount, asset, source, destination, purpose, and governing policy;
- signed intent, authorization, execution, and settlement receipts;
- trusted ordered timestamps;
- append-only hash-linked ledger entries;
- double-entry accounting records where applicable;
- reconciliation against banks, wallets, contracts, providers, or other rails;
- exceptions, reversals, disputes, and recovery history;
- encrypted redundant storage and tested restoration;
- audit-ready selective-disclosure evidence bundles.

The preferred descriptive terms are **proof of fiscal operation** and **proof of accountable operation**, not blockchain-mining “proof of work.”

Kindred may use verified internal operation to move through banking, payment-provider, partner, funding, compliance, marketplace, and expansion gates. No unsupported claim may be made under the Kindred name.
